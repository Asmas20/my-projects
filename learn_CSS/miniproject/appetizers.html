Appetizers

An appetizer, also known as a starter or hors d'oeuvre, is a small portion of food or drink served before a main course to stimulate the appetite. It can be simple or elaborate, including olives, bread, salads, or even small portions of hot or cold food.

Appetizers are a common feature in many cultures and are used to set the tone for the meal and enhance the dining experience.Appetizers have been part of culinary traditions for centuries in many cultures.

Here's a more detailed look at appetizers:

Stimulate appetite: Appetizers are designed to whet your appetite and prepare you for the main course.

Pace the meal: They provide a small amount of food to eat while waiting for the main courses, allowing the kitchen to prepare them.

Caprese salad bites, crispy calamari, spiced chicken satay skewers.

"warm hug for your taste buds, preparing them for the feast to come"----Nicholas

The term appetizer itself reflects its purpose of opening the appetite.

Enhance dining experience: Appetizers can showcase a chef's creativity and the restaurant's style.

Social and interactive: They can be shared, promoting conversation and interaction.
